# AI Platform Landscape Dashboard

## PURPOSE
The video lays out a whole market in one breath — Anthropic Co-work at 3/7, Google 4/7,
OpenAI 5/7, Microsoft 5.5/7, xAI Grokbot 1.5/7, and startups Kylon 6.5/7, Victor AI
5.5/7, Lindy 5/7, Open Claw 2.5/7 — and concludes the startups "have actually nearly
finished building a complete car." This Streamlit + Plotly dashboard makes that landscape
explorable: pick platforms, see which of the seven pieces (context, data, agents,
workspace, ledger, admin, custom apps) each one actually delivers, and compare the "lab
bet" against the "startup bet."

## WHY THIS MATTERS
It teaches why a single headline score hides the decision. The video stresses that no
platform has "shipped all seven pieces": Anthropic has the best workspace but no data
layer; Google's automations "only run on spreadsheets"; Open AI is the most complete lab.
A component-level visual is what turns those verdicts into a purchasing conversation.

## HOW TO BUILD
Streamlit for layout and Plotly for the charts. Keep the snapshot as a tidy DataFrame,
then render a radar chart for component profiles, a scatter of price-per-seat against
completeness, and a bar of the overall scores with a lab/startup colour split. Key
snippet:

```python
fig = go.Figure()
for name, row in df.iterrows():
    fig.add_trace(go.Scatterpolar(
        r=[row[c] for c in COMPONENTS] + [row[COMPONENTS[0]]],
        theta=COMPONENTS + [COMPONENTS[0]], name=name, fill="toself"))
fig.update_layout(title="Seven-piece coverage")
st.plotly_chart(fig, use_container_width=True)
```

Add a sidebar multiselect so only the platforms you care about are plotted, and a small
caption noting the adoption windows (startups 6–12 months, labs 12–18 months, now
shorter).

## WHEN TO USE IN PRODUCTION
Use it in a board deck, an agency's pitch, or an internal weekly market review — the
video is literally a "state of the market." Do NOT treat it as live truth: scores are one
reviewer's rubric in a fast-moving market ("the stack keeps changing"), and it must never
be the sole input to a platform commitment for regulated buyers who also need compliance
checks.

## HOW IT HELPS STUDENTS
You practise Streamlit widgets, session state, Plotly polar/Scatter trace building, and
turning a messy market into a decision-support view. Dashboards are among the most
requested portfolio pieces, and this one is grounded in a real transcript rather than toy
data.
